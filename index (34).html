Baofeng AR-5RM Programmer

Web Serial • AR-5RM / BF5RM protocol
Correct radio startup sequence:
  1. Turn the radio OFF.
  2. Connect the programming cable to the radio.
  3. Connect the USB cable to the computer.
  4. Click Connect Cable.
  5. Turn the radio ON.
  6. Click Detect AR-5RM.
The USB port being open does not mean the radio is detected. Detection requires the radio to answer PROGRAMBFNORMALU with 06.
